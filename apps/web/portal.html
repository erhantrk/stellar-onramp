Sandbox review outcome

Real KYC sandboxes let you pick a test scenario. The provider is mocked in this demo , but it verifies the answers you typed: the claims derive from your date of birth and country. Everything after this choice — the passkey wallet, the credential, the proof, the on-chain write — is real.

    TR mock anchor · Stellar testnet

    The gate contract says whether this wallet may buy; the anchor sells the USDC. The purchase runs SEP-10 authentication, a SEP-12 customer handoff with no documents, a SEP-38 firm quote and a SEP-6 bank deposit. The bank transfer is simulated by the sandbox anchor; the USDC it pays is real testnet USDC.

      Another application, reading the same record

      age-gated checkout · not part of this server

      A shop with two age-restricted shelves. It knows nothing about you: it calls check(subject, claim) on the gate contract from this browser and sells or refuses on the answer. The 21+ shelf refuses unless you disclosed over21, even though the same credential proves it — the chain only holds what you chose to reveal.

      To watch your own wallet get refused on the 18+ shelf, run verification again from the Verification tab with over18 unticked, or with a date of birth under 18. The record is rewritten and the shop's answer changes with it.
